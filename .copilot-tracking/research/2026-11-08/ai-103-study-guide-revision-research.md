# Research: AI-103 English study guide — October 31, 2026 revision and its predecessor

## Executive Summary

The Microsoft Learn AI-103 English study guide's visible **"Last updated on"** banner is confirmed, by direct live retrieval on 2026-11-08, to still read **2026-10-31** (`datetime="2026-10-31T01:16:00.000Z"`), matching issue #12's target revision exactly. The page's separate internal `<meta name="updated_at">` metadata field now reads `2026-11-05T22:08:00Z` — this is a **later, independent metadata timestamp, not the displayed "Last updated on" banner**, and it must not be treated as, or substituted for, the October 31 revision. The predecessor revision's banner date is recovered from two Wayback Machine captures (2026-06-06 and 2026-10-02) as **2026-04-14** (`datetime="2026-04-14T20:06:00.000Z"`), unchanged across both captures, meaning no revision occurred between at least June 6 and October 2, 2026. A direct line-level diff between the October 2, 2026 archived capture (predecessor state) and the live page (current, October 31 banner) found exactly three verified content edits, none of which touch the Skills Measured objectives, domains, or weightings. The "Skills measured as of April 16, 2026" objectives section is **byte-identical** between the predecessor and current revisions.

  The same page's `<head>` carries a separate `<meta name="updated_at" content="2026-11-05T22:08:00Z" />` and `<meta name="ms.date" content="2026-03-31T00:00:00Z" />`. These three dates (banner 2026-10-31, `updated_at` 2026-11-05, `ms.date` 2026-03-31) are three distinct fields that do **not** need to agree; only the banner (`data-article-date-source="calculated"`) is the human-facing "page last updated" claim the repository's `pageUpdatedOn`/`pageUpdatedAt` fields are built from (confirmed by the parser at `tools/exam-date-report.mjs:95-99`, which extracts `pageUpdatedOn`/`pageUpdatedAt` from the `Last updated on <local-time>` element and `metadataUpdatedAt` separately from `<meta name="updated_at">` at `tools/exam-date-report.mjs:101-102`).   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-103 (retrieved 2026-11-08, raw HTML).

* **F3 — Three verified, attributable content edits between the predecessor and current revisions; Skills Measured content unchanged.** A full text-line diff of the `<main>` article body (archived 2026-10-02 capture vs. live 2026-11-08 page, with Wayback's injected `/web/<timestamp>/` link-rewriting stripped before comparison) found exactly three differences:

Confidence: high — based on an automated line-by-line `Compare-Object` diff of stripped-tag text plus targeted raw-HTML substring checks; not a sampling/spot-check.
  Sources: both retrievals used above (predecessor 2026-10-02 capture; current live page 2026-11-08).

## Distinguishing page metadata vs. current vs. future objectives

* **Page metadata** (not an objectives signal): `ms.date` (2026-03-31, author-declared, unchanged across all observed revisions) and `updated_at` (an internal republish/metadata timestamp that has changed independently of, and later than, the visible banner — 2026-07-07 at the Oct 2 capture, then 2026-11-05 live). Neither of these two fields should be read as "the objectives changed."
* **Current objectives**: the guide shows one objectives version only, "Skills measured as of April 16, 2026," and this is presented as already in effect (not upcoming) in both the predecessor and current revisions. There is no second, "prior to," objectives block on this page (unlike AZ-104, which the repository's own `catalog/study-guide-dates.json` shows carries a "Skills measured prior to October 26, 2023" companion section — confirmed by `tools/fixtures/study-guide.html:7-8` and `catalog/study-guide-dates.json`'s AZ-104 entry).
* **Future/upcoming objectives**: none found for AI-103 in either revision. No forward-dated "Skills measured as of [future date]" text exists on the page as of this research.

## Gaps and uncertainties

* **No Wayback capture exists between 2026-10-02 and 2026-11-08.** The exact day the "Last updated on" banner changed from 2026-04-14 to 2026-10-31 cannot be independently pinpointed from Wayback Machine data; only the 2026-10-31 datetime value itself (as currently displayed live) is directly evidenced. It is possible (though unevidenced either way) that more than one edit occurred in that ~5-week window before the final 2026-10-31 banner was set; this research can only describe the net diff between the two end states (2026-10-02 predecessor capture and 2026-11-08 live page), not a guaranteed single-edit history.
* **No official Microsoft changelog, diff, or historical-objectives archive was found** for the AI-103 study guide. The `MicrosoftDocs/learn-certs-pr` GitHub repository named in the page's `original_content_git_url`/`gitcommit` metadata (`https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/learn-certs-pr`) returned HTTP 404 when fetched directly in this research, indicating it is private/internal and not usable as a public primary source for commit history. This matches the issue's own prior finding that no official historical comparison was recoverable; this research supplies an independent, Wayback-based comparison instead, not an official Microsoft one.
* **Text-level diff scope only.** The comparison in F3 covers the rendered article body's text and link targets; it does not independently verify non-textual aspects (e.g., whether linked destination pages such as `aka.ms/AI103-PA` or `aka.ms/AI103-ExamPrep` themselves changed, or whether anything server-side such as exam registration/scheduling changed). These are out of scope for "study guide page" revision comparison and are not claimed as verified either way.
* **Third-party mirrors (F6) are not primary sources.** The two third-party GitHub snapshots cited (`moonwalk5178/Azure-AI-Foundry-Wiki`, `jvargh/azure-ai-certs`) are unofficial transcriptions found via web search; they are cited only as independent corroboration that the April 16, 2026 effective date pre-dates the October 31, 2026 revision by many months, not as authoritative evidence of guide content.
* **"No substantive objective change" is a comparison-based negative finding, not an official Microsoft confirmation.** Microsoft has not published a statement that AI-103's objectives were unchanged on October 31, 2026; this research concludes "unchanged" solely because the two retrieved HTML documents' objectives sections are textually identical. Absence of a detected diff does not equal an official no-change attestation, consistent with the issue's own caution that "absence of a historical record is not proof that nothing changed" — here a historical record (Wayback capture) *was* recovered and *did* show no change in the objectives text specifically, which is a stronger basis than issue #12 had, but it is still not a Microsoft-sourced confirmation.
